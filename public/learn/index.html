Conversation5 min read

Live Interpreter vs Talk for Me

Both help in a conversation, but your role is different. Live interprets what people say. Talk for Me actively pursues an outcome for you.

Read guide

Use Live Interpreter when you are part of the conversation and want every side translated. You remain the person speaking and deciding.

Use Talk for Me when you can explain the goal first and want Ana to handle routine dialogue. Ana should return to you for material decisions such as prices, payments, appointments or changed conditions.

If you want to stay fully involved sentence by sentence, choose Live. If the communication itself is the burden, choose Talk for Me.

Listening4 min read

Live Subtitles vs Meeting Listen

They look similar on screen, but they solve different jobs: immediate understanding versus a long-session translated record.

Read guide

Live Subtitles are for “What are they saying right now?” They prioritize immediate subtitles and are temporary.

Meeting Listen is for Teams, Zoom and longer sessions. Ana processes short rolling segments, shows translated text and keeps the transcript on the device. Audio itself is not saved by Ana.

If you expect to review the conversation afterwards, use Meeting Listen.

Meetings6 min read

Using Ana beside Teams or Zoom

You do not need a Teams integration to start. Ana can sit beside the meeting in the browser and listen to shared computer audio or, when needed, the speakers through the microphone.

Read guide
  • On a desktop browser, prefer shared meeting/computer audio when the browser offers it.
  • If shared audio is unavailable, use Microphone / speakers and keep background noise low.
  • Choose the language you want to read before the meeting starts.
  • For long sessions use Meeting Listen rather than Live Subtitles so the translated transcript remains available.
  • Make sure the people involved are appropriately informed when speech is being processed.
Translation5 min read

Why meaning matters more than word-for-word translation

A sentence can be grammatically correct and still communicate the wrong intention. Ana is designed around the message the speaker is trying to convey.

Read guide

Literal translation often struggles with politeness, idioms, code-switching, professional terminology and sentences whose meaning depends on the surrounding conversation.

For important names, dates, amounts and reference numbers, the safest workflow is still to check the exact value. For recurring terminology, Ana’s personal glossary lets you keep a preferred translation.

Everyday life6 min read

Living or travelling in Germany without fluent German

Different situations need different levels of help. A restaurant menu and an appointment with an authority should not use the same workflow.

Read guide
  • Menu or sign: Camera.
  • Written letter: Documents.
  • Quick question in person: Live Interpreter.
  • A call where you know the outcome you need: Talk for Me.
  • Long work meeting: Meeting Listen.

For healthcare, legal, banking or authority matters, check critical facts yourself and follow the institution’s rules for interpreters and AI-assisted communication.

Trust & privacy7 min read

What Ana does with voice conversations

Voice translation requires speech to be processed while Ana is listening. That does not mean Ana needs to keep a recording.

Read guide

Ana’s voice modes are designed to separate processing from storage. Raw voice is not added to personal language memory. Personal memory is limited to items such as preferred language, glossary terms and privacy choices.

Meeting Listen keeps the translated text on the device but does not save the meeting audio. Talk for Me includes a transparency/permission boundary before it begins the external task.

Legal requirements vary by workplace, institution and country. Ana’s disclosure UI improves transparency; it is not a substitute for checking rules that apply to your specific situation.

Developers4 min read

Can another product use Ana?

Yes. Ana Platform packages translation, speech transcription and the trust pattern behind Talk for Me into server APIs for partner software.

Read guide

A partner product can use Ana’s translation and transcription endpoints directly from its backend. Talk for Me is exposed as a realtime session foundation with explicit owner-decision and critical-fact tools rather than as an unsafe “just do it” endpoint.

The developer API is private beta. It is an integration layer, not yet a ready-made Microsoft Teams bot.
