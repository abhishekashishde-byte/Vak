PRIVACY IS THE PRODUCT, TOO

Useful when you ask.
Quiet when you don't.

Ana is designed so using the keyboard does not mean continuously sending what you type to an AI service. Cloud features are separated from normal typing and protected by explicit controls.

01 · LOCAL FIRST

Normal typing is not a cloud request.

Key input, local suggestions, autocorrection, learned words and glide decoding can work on-device. Smart paragraph correction is a separate opt-in feature.

02 · PRIVATE FIELDS

Passwords and OTPs are treated differently.

Ana disables AI behaviour in sensitive fields. Clipboard and voice features are also kept away from private input contexts.

03 · PRIVACY SHIELD

Protect sensitive values before AI.

When enabled, Ana can mask recognised personal values before cloud AI processing and restore them afterwards.

04 · USER CONTROL

Incognito and per-app controls actually change behaviour.

Incognito pauses AI, learning and clipboard history. AI actions can also remain disabled in apps where you do not want them.

Reply does not read your screen. Ana uses text you deliberately select, copy or place in the editor. It does not silently scrape surrounding WhatsApp, Gmail or other app content.
