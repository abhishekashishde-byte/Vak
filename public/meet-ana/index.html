PRIVACY

Useful when you ask.
Quiet when you don’t.

Ana separates normal typing from cloud AI instead of treating every keystroke as an AI request.

LOCAL FIRST

Normal typing stays local.

Basic typing, suggestions, autocorrection, learned words and glide do not need a cloud AI round-trip.

PRIVATE CONTEXTS

Sensitive fields are treated differently.

Password and OTP-style fields disable Ana AI behaviour and keep voice and clipboard features away.

YOU CONTROL AI

AI is a choice.

Privacy Shield, Incognito and per-app controls decide when Ana is allowed to use cloud features.

Reply does not read your screen. Ana only uses text you deliberately select, copy or place in the editor.
